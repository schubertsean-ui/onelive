# Beachhead sector — decision memo (FOR THE FOUNDER; no decision taken here)

**Why one sector first:** the market analysis (§13) recommends narrow-and-deep
entry — coverage SLOs, sector-tuned claim taxonomies, and golden-set

## Selection criteria (from the analysis + schema work)

1. **Promise density** — releases carry many checkable forward commitments.
2. **Fulfillment observability** — outcomes are public and datable (approvals,
   launches, filings), so verdicts can reach `confirmed` without judgment calls.
3. **Cadence regularity** — silence detection (H8) needs a rhythm to model.
4. **EDGAR coverage** — the free backbone must carry most of the sector's
   material communications (US-listed heavy).
5. **Buyer adjacency** — the sector's followers include chasm-tier funds and
   consultants who already pay for information.
6. **Legal blast radius** — early extraction errors should not be
   catastrophic (avoid starting where a wrong verdict moves a stock 40%… which
   cuts against the most tempting option below — stated, not hidden).

## Candidates (reported characteristics; verify at greenlight)

| Sector | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| **Biotech / small-cap pharma** | Highest promise density (trial milestones, FDA dates); outcomes exquisitely observable (approvals are public events); catalyst-driven investor base overlaps multibagger's hunting ground | Highest blast radius — a wrong "trial promise broken" verdict on a $300M biotech is the defamation nightmare scenario; claim language is technical |
| **Consumer tech / hardware** | Launch promises are dated and famous; outcomes visible in stores; large retail/prosumer audience | Fulfillment often partial ("shipped but late/cut-down") — verdicts get judgment-heavy; promise density moderate |
| **Space / defense-adjacent growth** | Launch manifests and delivery dates are public and datable; passionate investor following; moderate universe size | Government-contract opacity for some outcomes; smaller buyer base |
| **Banks / financials** | Guidance-rich, regular cadence, dense EDGAR coverage | Guidance tracking here is closest to what Visible Alpha/Marvin already do — least whitespace |

## Recommendation posture

The strongest *product* fit is biotech (criteria 1–5) and the strongest
*risk* fit is consumer tech or space (criterion 6). One resolution worth
weighing: launch biotech with verdicts capped at `likely` (never `confirmed`/
`broken`) until the golden set proves precision on real filings — the 4-state
model exists precisely so caution can be graduated. Your call; recording it
will trigger the sector po battery + friction attack per charter.
