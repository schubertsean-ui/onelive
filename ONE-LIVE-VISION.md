## The world we are building

A world where every live activity is easy to find, fairly represented, and culturally valued.
At scale: let culture grow without being stripped of its soul.
1Live is the ethical heartbeat of culture.

## What 1Live is

A system of record for what’s really on — any category, any locale, person, organization, group, activity, artist, performance, and place-first — a happening.

## What 1Live accurately publishes

Every activity anywhere in the world.

## Goals (how Vision is achieved)

1. Answer tonight in under 10 seconds.
2. Every real activity findable. Discovery never for sale.
3. Time given back.
4. Listed by default if it is on.
5. Places appear from activity, not payment.
6. Social validates, never defines.
7. No sponsored discovery.
8. Heartbeat is city pulse, not a person.

## The lock

- Map, not shop.
- Every category, any locale.
- No category weighting.
- Publishers trusted until wrong.
- On-device plans.
- Heartbeat = de-identified pulse.
- Beautiful + automagical.
- Trust serves the vision.
