## The three directions (self-contained HTML, open in any browser)

| File | Name | Lean (PART B) | Signature element |
|---|---|---|---|
| `direction-1-marquee.html` | **MARQUEE** | warm / analog-human | The Doorlight — uniform tungsten glow on each card's leading edge; the feed as a street of slightly-open doors |
| `direction-2-current.html` | **CURRENT** | electric / kinetic | The Pulse — one citywide heartbeat line under the masthead (never per-listing) + current-cut card corner |
| `direction-3-setlist.html` | **SETLIST** | editorial / monumental | The Time Rule + Edition Masthead — the night as a dated printed setlist ("No. 197"), gone tomorrow |

All three include the three mandated screens (Tonight feed · Filter panel ·
Event Detail), dark mode rendered + light palette specified, and honor the
ratified trust-display rules: **no badges/"verified"/"confirmed" anywhere**;
uncertainty = one quiet icon + one-tap-dismissible sheet with the venue's own
site linked; Spark Lines with attribution incl. the tier-C machine-drafted
register (italic, one shade quieter, small ✳, "— first notes", same
dismissible-sheet pattern); Emotion Glyphs as self-rendered inline SVGs with
aria-labels (no platform emoji, no banned rating-glyphs, uniform placement so
no visible hierarchy between listings); verbatim copy strings ("Tonight in
Austin", "Less chaos. Real shows.", "Something off?"); WCAG 2.2 AA contrast
targets and ≥44px touch targets; date tabs; Free/Ticketed; "Hear it".
Fixture artists are FICTIONAL (no facts asserted about real acts); Austin
venue names appear as setting only. Typography uses licensed-face
specifications (Fraunces / Space Grotesk / Archivo) with system fallbacks in
the mockups — faces are licensed at implementation, not embedded here.

## PART C rubric — generator self-scores (honest; the founder's scoring decides)

| Criterion (1–5) | MARQUEE | CURRENT | SETLIST |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · 10-second answer on the feed | 4 | **5** | 4 |
| 2 · Night-sidewalk legibility | 4 | **5** | 4 |
| 3 · Trust-by-craft (zero badges, feels dependable) | **5** | 4 | **5** |
| 4 · Distinctiveness vs named competitors | 4 | 4 | **5** |
| 5 · Emotional fidelity (anticipation + calm certainty) | **5** | 4 | 4 |
| 6 · Accessibility evident in comps | 4 | **5** | 3 |
| 7 · Survivability (year 3, other cities, light mode) | 4 | 4 | **5** |
| 8 · Click-pull (curiosity gap + white-hat reflection test) | **5** | 4 | 4 |
| **Total** | **35** | **35** | **34** |

Honest notes: CURRENT wins pure utility (time-first cards, highest contrast,
tabular numerals) but runs closest to "energetic promo" territory — its
discipline is the single Pulse line. MARQUEE is the most literal translation
of the brief's §3 sidewalk moment and the strongest door/curiosity metaphor;
its risk is drifting toward nostalgia-kitsch if the warmth is overdone at
implementation. SETLIST is the most ownable and ages best (and is the only
direction whose light mode is a structural advantage, not a port); its known
weakness is scored honestly — condensed uppercase + italic serif needs
careful sizing to hold AA and dyslexia-friendly reading, fixable at
implementation. Scores deliberately not tied: 35/35/34 reflects real
tradeoffs, and the rubric's tie-breaker (signature strength) then favors
whichever signature the founder finds most ownable.
