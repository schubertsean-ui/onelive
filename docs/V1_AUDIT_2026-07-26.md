## The verdict in six sentences

The engineering underneath is genuinely strong — 1,665 passing tests, a real trust
architecture, careful credential handling, deterministic importers, an independent
non-Claude reviewer that has caught real attacks. But the build had been optimising the
*process of building* rather than the *product*, and at audit time both data pipelines
were broken: the local-venue feed had **never once run on its schedule** (a one-line
workflow bug, D1), and extraction was off because the Anthropic **spend cap** was hit —
a console setting, not a date to wait out. The single most important structural finding:
the founder's 2026-07-25 auto-publish decision **was recorded, a policy module written
and tested — and never wired to anything** (D3), so a long-tail event still cannot reach
the site without a human click, the exact thing that does not scale.

## 5. Where the weight actually is, and what to cut

The read-before-you-code surface went from **11,770 words across 7 documents to 10,068
across 4** — a **14%** word reduction (an earlier draft wrongly implied a cut to a third;
corrected here, since overstating it would be the exact false-confidence class this audit
names). `CLAUDE.md` was rewritten **from 2,834 words** to a short binding contract that
opens with the vision rather than the rules, every invariant kept at full strength.

**The vision-first correction, and the sharper finding it produced.** The founder's
2026-07-26 directive exposed that `docs/BAR.md` had shipped with 66 rows grading the
machine and **zero rows naming the vision, the mission, the promise to the fan, or the
feeling** — a bigger defect than any word count. Fixed: BAR opens with §0 (the vision,
quoted) and section **P**, fourteen rows on purpose and felt experience, ahead of every
engineering section (record: `docs/memory/decisions/2026-07-26_vision-first-directive.md`).
The finding that fell out: **six of fourteen P rows are met, against forty-five of
sixty-six engineering rows — the machine is in far better shape than the experience it
exists to deliver, and P1, the ten-second answer, has never been measured once.**

**What changed (this PR), and what deliberately did not.** `CLAUDE.md` rewritten (vision
first; the extraction-exception essay moved to `docs/EXTRACTION_EXCEPTION.md` where its
mechanism lives). `docs/HOW_WE_WORK.md` replaces the working surface of five process docs
(originals kept on disk as reference). `docs/BAR.md` and `docs/INDEX.md` are new — the bar
as numbers-per-aspect, and every document classified CANON / REFERENCE / HISTORICAL.
**Nothing mechanical was cut:** no gate threshold, no test, no `tools/validate` check, no
invariant. Simplification meant fewer words, not fewer gates.

## 6. Are the Loops, the Harness and the Brain working?

Working well: the gate (19 checks, fails closed, refuses to count a SKIP as a pass — the
strongest part of the system), the two-seat independent reviewer, contract-first. Not
working: small batches (`commit_sweep` reported 10 of the last 15 commits oversized, into
an ADVISORY column nobody had to act on); the reconciler (cannot verify from the sandbox,
D6); harness self-pruning (a WORLD_CLASS §0.8 obligation, first performed in this PR); the
environment (D7/D12). The Brain is effectively unwired — 5,393 lines, two nodes stored
(D9); the working memory is the plain-text `docs/memory/` directory, which does work.

**The bottleneck, named** (§0.9 requires it): no longer coding, planning or verification —
**it is delivery.** The gates are excellent, the plans thorough, and the product still
cannot show a long-tail event without a human click.
