# 1LIVE — Card rooms (founder paste 2026-09-08)

**Status:** RATIFIED. This is the card the live site must show.
**Wins over** UI Canon §2 where they disagree: the card is **two rooms**, not one thin row.
Canon file remains `docs/design/ONE_LIVE_TONIGHT_UI_CANON_v1.md` for principles, spark, glyph, trust, lens, density.

Nothing invented to fill a hole. First look is a hook. The rest opens on tap.

## Around the cards

- Place name and honest count
- Date tabs: Today / Tomorrow / This week / All
- Filters: kind, genre, area, Free (slide-in, one quiet entry)
- Ask and Plan (plan stays on the device)
- Evening first, then earlier
- Map, not shop. No pay-to-rank. No “verified” badge

## At rest — artist room

1. Artist or act photo (event `image_url` only; no stand-in portrait)
2. Name or title
3. Time line: start · doors · price (Free is mint) · on now
4. Spark line — 3, 5, or 7 words. What it may be like and how it may feel
5. Emotion glyph — weather, not a rating (empty until a creator description exists)
6. Preview hook — Hear them / Watch a talk / See a set / trailer / the works / the room
7. Kind chip — Live Music, Comedy, Markets, Family, Sports, Classes… plus subgenre when a desk printed one
8. Quiet “?” only when details are thin

## At rest — venue room

9. Venue photo — empty this ticket (no venue_photo field)
10. Venue name
11. Character line — what the space is like. Never slighting. Never invented glow. Empty until printed.
12. Mini-map chip — printed area or city only. No fake miles.
13. Street address as text on the card · map link lives in the lens
14. From the venue — their specials, attributed. Never changes rank. Empty until printed.
15. See nearby — empty until we have other venues with activities
16. Venue’s own site as host text on the card · real link lives in the lens

## On tap — artist/org/event door

Photo, genres, preview or player, how long it runs. Link to the respective site.

## On tap — venue door

Photo, character, address, distance, specials, nearby walk ring, site, phone, parking when printed.

## Full page

- When, where, kind
- Check the venue (their site / their phone)
- Tickets ↗ — we send people out. We do not sell
- Add to calendar · Share
- How we know / via
- Disputed stays visible (“sources disagree”)
- If title, place, or topic is still empty: “We’re looking for more information. Check or call the site, artist, or organizer to confirm details.”

## How dense the card is

- This week: full card (two rooms)
- Later this month: compact row
- Beyond a month: date · act · venue · price

## Honest holes this ticket does not invent

Doors time, venue photo, character line, specials, nearby, emotion glyph, distance, parking. Slots exist. Empty stays empty.
