## Block A — foundation (paste FIRST in every chat)

Design a mobile-first event-discovery feed screen called "Tonight in Austin"
for a product named 1LIVE. It answers "what's happening tonight?" in under
ten seconds, no account, no onboarding. Laws, non-negotiable: (1) Calm over
clutter — a card shows a few things beautifully; depth is one tap away, never
piled on the surface. (2) Cards are "slightly-open doors" — one curiosity
hook each, never a summary. (3) NO trust badges of any kind — never
"verified/confirmed/trusted," no shields, checkmarks, or stars; a trustworthy
listing is simply clean; uncertainty is ONE small quiet icon that opens a
small dismissible sheet in calm language; a disputed event shows a quiet
"sources disagree" note, never hidden. (4) No sponsored placement, no visual
hierarchy that could read as ranking-for-money. (5) Honest gaps: if data is
missing show nothing, never filler. (6) WCAG 2.2 AA, tap targets ≥44px,
works in light and dark.

Card anatomy (one card = one "room" with two tappable doors): ARTIST zone —
artist name headline, one time row (start time · price pill, mint for Free ·
"on now" tag), a 3-to-7-word vivid "spark line" hook (fragments allowed:
"brass. menace. amen."), a small preview affordance ("▸ Hear them"); VENUE
zone — its own slightly-darker block: venue name + one-line character line
("basement jazz cellar, candle-dark, standing"), area/distance. Each zone
ends in a quiet "artist ›" / "venue ›" cue. Below the card row: a "How we
know" affordance naming the real source ("Reviewed and published from Mohawk
Austin · See the source's site ↗").

Feed structure: masthead "1LIVE · Tonight in Austin" + honest count line
("57 shown · by category, soonest first"); date tabs (Today / Tomorrow /
weekday names / All); ONE quiet "Filters" entry that slides in
category/genre/area/free-only chips; the river groups by cultural domain
(Live Music, Comedy, Theater, Visual Arts, Food & Drink…), each group
hue-tinted, events soonest-first inside. Show 6–8 example cards with
realistic Austin venue names and varied categories. Include both a rich card
tier and a compact one-line row tier for later-dated events.

## Block B — one direction per chat (three chats)

**Chat 1 — "Night Editorial":** Render the foundation as a nighttime
newspaper — disciplined typographic hierarchy, serif display for act names,
generous whitespace, ink-dark ground with warm paper accents, hairline rules
between domain groups. The feeling: a beautifully typeset evening edition
you trust because it's composed, not decorated.

**Chat 2 — "Analog Warmth":** Render the foundation with the texture of
Austin's physical music culture — gig-poster influence, slightly tactile
surfaces, warm amber/rust accents on deep brown-black, rounded photo tiles,
hand-set-feeling labels. Warm, human, a little worn-in; never kitsch, never
cluttered.

**Chat 3 — "Liquid Dark":** Render the foundation as a calm, luminous dark
interface — deep near-black ground, one restrained electric accent per
domain hue, soft glass panels for the venue blocks, glow used only for
"on now." Feels fast, fluid, and quiet; motion implied by composition, not
animation.
