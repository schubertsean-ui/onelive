## 3. GAPS — specific, step-by-step, owner named

### GAP A — the 131 AI-extract sources publish nothing (the moat, and the biggest gap)
**Sources:** 131 — every Hill Country winery/brewery/distillery, and venues like
`stubbs_austin`, `saxon_pub`, `continental_club`, `white_horse`, `paramount_austin`,
`zach_theatre`, `emos`, `scoot_inn`, `mohawk_austin`, museums, festivals. (Full list
in the matrix.)
**Why zero:** AI extracts candidates every 20 min, but the **triangulation +
auto-publish engine is unbuilt** — `worker/publish_policy.py` (the decision layer)
exists and is ratified, but its promoter is a **pending**, **future** build absent
from the tree today (`worker/autopromote.py` was never created), and
`decide_publish()` is called by nothing. So candidates never become published events.
**Step-by-step (ME):**
1. Build the **pending** `worker/autopromote.py` (a **future** file) — it will run
   `decide_publish` inside the loop; **flag stays fail-closed OFF**.
2. Build the **triangulation matcher** — corroborate each AI find against licensed
   rows, ICS/feed/calendar-platform sources, venue social posts, and gov venue-truth
   (Gap E). Agreement raises confidence unverified→likely→confirmed; contradiction→disputed (shown, never hidden).
3. Wire **source-reliability grading** + verify the **honest uncertainty display** on the feed.
4. **Adversarial review** (trust path), then earned-confidence publishing goes live.
**Step-by-step (YOU):** nothing to build; when I show proof the safeguards are live, you confirm your standing 2026-07-25 ratification. **I show proof first.**
**By when:** the engine is my top build after the feed adapters below; I'll land it as its own reviewed PR and report progress on completion, not on a timer.
