# ONE LIVE — The Promotion Studio v1 (proactive, event-triggered campaign kits for THEIR channels)

**Compiled 2026-08-01 · Status: PROPOSAL — research response to the founder's 2026-08-01 directive: an additional service that actually promotes the venue/event/activities — ad and content creation, design, posting execution (carousels etc.), and measurement — clarified: NOT on OneLive; via their social channels ("if we know they have a wine tasting coming up we could spin up ads and a carousel for them to run in FB and IG"). Companion to the four-doc Owned Agent package (PR #48). Questions Q21–Q22. Nothing here is license to build.**

Greppable summary: the Promotion Studio = event-triggered, proactively
generated CAMPAIGN KITS (carousel + story crops + flyer + per-platform
captions + simple geo ad recipe + schedule + plain-language measurement)
for the business's OWN channels and ad accounts — the agent already knows
the event from the F1 calendar spine, so the kit arrives BEFORE they ask.
Market basis: full-service social agencies charge $1k–$5k/mo for venue
packages whose event-promotion core is templated work over data we already
hold (the arbitrage table below). Hard guardrails: their channels, their
ad accounts and budgets, their tap is ALWAYS the send button (owner
publishes — preserved physics posture), and ZERO interplay with OneLive
ranking or display — promoting never buys visibility on OneLive, at any
price, for anyone. Two execution paths: v1 needs NO Meta API (kit + 2-tap
boost recipe run in their own app); assisted execution via their
authorized ad account is the Phase-C upgrade behind Meta review. Pricing
posture is a founder decision (Q21): capped-free growth weapon vs low
flat fee vs decide-at-Phase-B. Extends F5 and refines §14.2 of the
assessment — recorded as a posture amendment, not an invariant change.

## 2. The mechanic: proactive, event-triggered campaign kits

The differentiating move is not "we also do social" — it is that the agent
ALREADY KNOWS. The F1 calendar spine means we see the wine tasting twelve
days out. So the Studio inverts the agency workflow:

> Agency model: business remembers → briefs agency → waits → reviews →
> pays $3k/mo whether or not anything happened this month.
>
> Studio model: **the kit shows up.** "Your wine tasting is in 12 days.
> Here's your carousel, your captions, your ad recipe, your posting
> schedule. Approve, edit, or ignore."

**A campaign kit contains:**
- **Creative:** announcement carousel (3–6 cards), story-sized crops,
  a one-tap flyer/poster render — built from the event's real data
  (name, date, time, art/photos they've provided or published) in the
  business's own look (colors/logo captured once at claim time).
- **Copy:** per-platform captions in their voice (short IG, longer FB,
  event-page text), hashtags/geo-tags. Editable, regenerable.
- **Ad recipe:** the simple high-value ad ("people within X miles with
  interests matching this event, this week, $Y cap") expressed as either
  (a) a 2-tap boost recipe they execute in their own Meta app — **v1,
  no Meta API needed** — or (b) a pre-built ad set pushed to THEIR ad
  account via authorized API access — Phase-C upgrade, behind Meta app
  review (the same review reality documented in OWNED_AGENT §3).
- **Schedule:** when each piece should go out (announce / reminder /
  day-of), pre-staged so "approve" = scheduled to their connected
  channels; every send is owner-tapped or owner-scheduled — the agent
  never posts on its own initiative.
- **Measurement:** trackable links and door/promo codes generated per
  campaign; afterwards a plain-language result ("1,200 saw it, 340
  tapped, 41 used the code; the carousel beat the flyer 3-to-1 — lead
  with carousels next time?") that feeds the next kit. Measurement is
  the retention loop: it is how the Studio LEARNS their audience.

## 3. What the best firms deliver, and the arbitrage

Full-service social packages for small businesses run
[$1,000–$5,000/month, mid-tier $2,500–$5,000 covering content creation,
community management, and ads](https://newmedia.com/blog/social-media-marketing-cost)
([tier breakdown](https://socialrails.com/blog/social-media-marketing-agency-pricing-guide);
[what's included](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/social-media-management-cost/)).
The menu: strategy, monthly content calendar (20–30 posts), branded
graphics/carousels, short-video editing, captions, scheduling/publishing,
flyers, boosted posts and geo-targeted ads (typically +10–20% of spend),
email blasts, review responses, monthly reporting.

**The not-hard-but-expensive core (the Studio's target):**

| Billed as | Reality | Typical price | Studio cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Custom design per asset (carousel, flyer, story crops) | Template + data we already hold + their photos | $100–$500/asset | ~cents |
| Captions + content calendar | Their calendar restated warmly, 30×/month | inside $1–3k retainers | ~cents |
| Scheduling + cross-platform resizing | Solved software problem billed as labor | inside retainers | ~$0 |
| Monthly report | Repackaged platform analytics + commentary | $300–$1k of retainer | ~$0 (automated, plainer) |
| Basic geo event ads | A form: radius + interests + dates + budget | 10–20% of ad spend | $0 fee (their account, their budget, no percentage) |

**What is GENUINELY hard and we don't pretend to do:** original
photography/videography, brand invention, community management with human
judgment, real creative strategy. Named in the pitch and left,
respectfully, to the §14 REFER world — this keeps the Studio's claim
honest and the consultant ecosystem unthreatened where they add real value.

## 4. Hard guardrails (what makes this compatible with everything we've said)

1. **Never on OneLive.** The Studio operates exclusively on the
   business's own channels and ad accounts. Nothing the Studio does —
   free or paid — affects OneLive ranking, ordering, display, or trust
   states in any way. "Being seen more is for sale (on your channels).
   Being ranked higher (on ours) never is, for anyone, at any price."
   This is the no-pay-to-rank invariant's Studio corollary, stated so it
   can be ratified rather than assumed.
2. **The owner's tap is always the send button.** The agent prepares and
   stages; the owner approves/schedules; nothing auto-posts. This
   PRESERVES the F5 physics posture (owner publishes) while extending
   its scope from "drafts" to "full staged campaigns" — the posture
   amendment this doc records (supersedes the narrower F5 wording in
   OWNED_AGENT §5 and refines B2A_ASSESSMENT §14.2's "paid media NEVER":
   what remains NEVER is fee-skimmed spend management, ad brokering,
   commissions, and any OneLive-surface interplay; what changes is that
   generating and staging campaigns for THEIR channels is now in scope).
3. **Their money stays theirs.** Ads run on their ad account with their
   budget cap; we take no percentage of spend (the 10–20% skim is the
   agency model's misaligned incentive — our incentive is their event
   working, not their spend growing).
4. **Artist framing rules apply** (OWNED_AGENT Part II §14): for artists
   the Studio is presented as "your announcement kit," never generative
   AI making their art; their photos, their voice settings, full edit
   control; nothing publishes without them.
5. **Data boundary:** kits are generated from the entity's own data and
   assets plus what they explicitly connect. Consumer-side OneLive data
   never feeds ad targeting (consistent with open+private Q8 posture).

## 5. Cost and pricing posture (founder decision Q21)

**Our COGS per kit:** asset generation + copy ≈ cents at the
cheapest-capable tier (deterministic templating + small generation calls);
measurement ≈ $0 (links/codes + platform metrics they connect). A venue
running 8 events/month costs us well under $1 — the Studio inherits the
free tier's economics, unlike the Mirror's scan costs.

**Three postures (present, not decided):**
- **(a) Free with caps** — the growth weapon: N kits/month included.
  Strongest adoption + mantle coherence ("even the megaphone is free");
  forfeits the natural revenue line; caps guard COGS and spam.
- **(b) Low flat fee** (e.g. $19–$49/mo unlimited kits) — the first
  non-rank revenue: undercuts agencies ~100×, funds the free tier,
  cleanly separated from basics ("basics free forever; the megaphone is
  optional"). Requires Stripe billing (new-service founder decision) and
  care that the paid line NEVER creeps toward visibility on OneLive.
- **(c) Defer** — ship nothing paid; decide at Phase B with real usage.

Recommendation: **(c) formally now, designed so (a) or (b) can switch on
without rework** — pricing is a Phase-B decision with data; what must be
decided NOW (Q21) is only that the Studio direction is ratified and which
posture the build should keep open.
