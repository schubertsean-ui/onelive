## Harvest (traceable)

- **H1 (P1: "no adjudicator")** — adjudication yields a POSITION plus
  PRESERVED DISSENTS: minority assessments stay in the record with
  their rationale, never averaged away (extends the ratified Blue-hat
  conflict-preserving merge to data assessment).
- **H2 (P1: "ambiguity is the product")** — ambiguity is itself a
  MEASURED SIGNAL: category-membership entropy and assessor
  disagreement become displayable/analyzable values ("the scene splits
  on what this show is") feeding cultural analytics, not merely costs
  to minimize.
- **H3 (P2 reversal: the event assesses the sources)** — resolution
  grades everyone: when an event resolves (happened/didn't/changed),
  every source and assessor that opined gets scored — reputation flows
  BACKWARD into per-source reliability priors (joins the social-
  composite spec's agreement-weights).
- **H4 (P3 exaggeration: re-run every second)** — re-adjudication is
  EVENT-TIME-INDEXED with escalating density (sparse at T-30d, dense at
  T-24h) plus TRIGGERED by falsifier/new-source arrival — never
  clock-uniform.
- **H5 (P4 distortion: adjudicate before assessing)** — PRIORS FIRST +
  Value-of-Information gating: a new claim inherits category/venue/
  source base rates instantly; the swarm convenes only when the prior
  is both uncertain AND decision-relevant (would the display decision
  change?). Token spend follows decision value.
- **H6 (P5 wishful: perfect future knowledge)** — the CLOSURE LOOP:
  post-event verification is cheap (did it happen? setlist echo,
  social echo, next newsletter) and every resolved outcome becomes
  calibration ground truth. The system's accuracy is measured against
  resolutions, not vibes.
- **H7 (P6 absurd: claims hold a parliament)** — taken straight:
  every assessment must NAME ITS FALSIFIERS ("what would change my
  mind"); the adjudicator subscribes to falsifier arrival — this
  mechanizes "learning what to search for next."
- **H8 (P7 riverbed: sediment strata)** — assessments are STRATA,
  never overwrites: each adjudication run deposits a layer (position,
  rationale, inputs-hash, time-to-event); strata are the audit trail
  AND the calibration dataset.
- **H9 (P7 riverbed: dry season reveals the bed)** — quiet periods log
  cheap NO-CHANGE AFFIRMATIONS so "examined, unchanged" is
  distinguishable from "never looked."
- **H10 (P7 riverbed shapes the flow)** — the CULTURAL HEARTBEAT is
  the slow layer: genre momentum, venue vitality, neighborhood pulse
  computed from RESOLVED strata (post-event verified ground), with H2's
  ambiguity metrics as first-class cultural signals.
- **H11 (P8: the flood redraws the bed)** — REGIME-CHANGE detection:
  when resolutions repeatedly surprise the priors (calibration drift),
  the affected base rates re-learn LOUDLY (Kaizen row, never silent
  adaptation).
