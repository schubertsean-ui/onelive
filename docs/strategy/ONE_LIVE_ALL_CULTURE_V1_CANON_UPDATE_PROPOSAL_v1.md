# ONE LIVE — All-Culture v1 Canon Update (PROPOSAL for founder ratification)

**Status: PROPOSAL.** This drafts the *actual replacement language* to fold the
founder's current direction (2026-07-29) into the ratified canon, which still carries
stale **music-first / "expansion-earned"** framing. Per charter, ratified founder text
is **proposed, never unilaterally rewritten** — so this doc puts the new wording in
front of you to approve, edit, or reject item by item. On ratification, the edits are
applied to the named docs in one pass.

**Nothing here relaxes a trust invariant or a gate.** It generalizes scope
(music → all culture), sharpens positioning (anti-attention / least Total Attention
Cost), and raises the ceiling (the Heartbeat "cultural intelligence for thriving"
vision). The invariants — no-pay-to-rank, AI-never-publishes, disputed-shown,
aggregate-only, consent-gated, no surveillance — are **reaffirmed unchanged** (§7).

Companion: `ONE_LIVE_ALL_CULTURE_V1_STRATEGIC_ANALYSIS_v1.md` (the why).

---

## 0. The through-line of the change

**From:** "a live-music discovery platform launching in Austin."
**To:** *the open, trust-first, least-attention system of record for **everything**
happening in a community — all culture and entertainment — that helps people get the
information they want, do the things they want, and live the life they want; and, over
time, the cultural-intelligence layer that helps communities, their culture, and the
people in them thrive and flourish.*

Two altitudes, and the update must hold both:
- **v1, the practical wedge (now):** find the good thing across *all* culture, know
  what it's like, plan the night, go — at the least possible cost to your attention.
- **The ambition (the mountain):** unparalleled data, insight, and actionable content
  for individuals, organizations, and cities — all pointed at **thriving**. (Directional
  vision; each surface a later, separately-gated, founder-crucial build.)

## 5. Anti-attention / Total Attention Cost — add as a first-class principle

*(New principle for `ONE_LIVE_PRODUCT_VISION_AND_PRINCIPLES_v1.md` Core Principles, and
already grounded in design brief §2 "Automagic" and §6 behavioral architecture, and in
`ONE_LIVE_FRICTIONLESS_NAV_v1.md`.)*

> **Least Total Attention Cost (canonical principle).** ONE LIVE is measured by how
> *little* of a person's attention it costs to deliver the most real-world value — not
> by time-on-app. We optimize time-to-answer, taps-to-plan, and decided-plan rate
> *down*, and we **never** optimize dwell or session length. Rich context is delivered
> at the point of decision so depth **lowers** the cost of attention rather than adding
> to it. We give attention **back**, so people go live their lives. Trust is
> foundational — who we are — not an attribute; these behavioral mechanics are white-hat
> only, and safe to use *because* trust is foundational.

Add the framing pair as canon: **practical value now, flourishing later.**

## 6. Heartbeat → "Cultural Intelligence for Thriving" — proposed vision expansion

*(Expands the "Heartbeat Analytics" future-surface entry in
`ONE_LIVE_PRODUCT_VISION_AND_PRINCIPLES_v1.md`. This is **directional vision** — each
tier below is a later, separately-gated, founder-crucial build; nothing here is live,
and every tier is bound by the trust invariants in §7 from day one.)*

This is the mountain: not "city dashboards," but **the cultural-intelligence layer that
helps communities, their culture, and the people in them thrive** — built on data no
one else has (all-culture, resolved-truth, provenance-clean, emotion-aware, first-party
+ public-agency). Unparalleled *because* of the trust architecture, not despite it.

> **Heartbeat — Cultural Intelligence for Thriving (vision).** Over time, ONE LIVE
> turns the truth it assembles about a community's culture into **unparalleled data,
> insight, and actionable content**, served to those who can use it to help a community
> and its people flourish — always **aggregate-only, consent-gated, never individual
> resale, never sold surveillance, never pay-to-rank.** Four stakeholder tiers:
>
> - **Individuals (opt-in, personal, on-device-first):** "live the life you want" —
>   actionable, anticipatory help finding and doing the things that enrich *your* life
>   (the concierge lens). A lens, never a gate; declared preference, never inferred
>   surveillance; you own your data.
> - **Creators & organizers (their own data + aggregate context):** understand and
>   grow your audience and your scene — honest demand signals, what's rising, how your
>   work travels — tooling that helps culture-makers thrive.
> - **Groups & organizations (aggregate):** neighborhoods, cultural nonprofits,
>   BIDs, tourism, hospitality — insight into the cultural life around them to program,
>   partner, and invest well.
> - **Cities & municipalities (aggregate, civic):** measure and grow the **cultural
>   economy and cultural vibrancy** — density, momentum, equity of access, gaps to fill
>   — the honest, provenance-clean measurement no walled garden can provide (it only
>   sees its own inventory).
>
> **Why unparalleled:** it consumes *resolved* post-event ground truth across *all*
> culture, with provenance and emotion — a dataset that can grade itself against
> reality and that no attention-economy player can reconstruct (their coverage is a
> monetizable slice; ours is the whole community). **Why trustworthy:** the same
> invariants that govern the product govern the data — aggregate-only, consent-gated,
> and the moment it touched pay-to-rank or non-aggregate resale it would trip a
> dissolution trigger.

The purpose line, canonical: **the point of the intelligence is thriving — of
individuals, of culture, of communities — not extraction.**

## 7. What does NOT change (reaffirmed invariants)

The update generalizes and elevates; it relaxes nothing. Unchanged and binding:
- **No pay-to-rank**, ever (dissolution trigger).
- **AI never publishes** — the extraction→gate→promote pipeline; a human-custodied gate.
- **Disputed shown, never hidden**; the 4-state confidence model.
- **Aggregate-only insights; no artist/individual-level data resale without consent**
  (dissolution trigger); **no surveillance**, numbers/PII never sold.
- **RLS fail-closed; trust as physics, not policy.**
- **Personalization / anticipation is a lens, never a gate.**
