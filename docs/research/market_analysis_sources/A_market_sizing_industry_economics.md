**Structural takeaway (for report framing):** issuers pay $100-$8,700 per release (public companies with disclosure obligations pay the premium Business Wire/PR Newswire/GlobeNewswire tiers); the three major wires' corporate parents are respectively Berkshire (stable), Platinum Equity (distressed debt, Caa1), and Equiniti (just acquired for $534.5M) — the ecosystem is funded by issuers who *want* maximum distribution, i.e., press releases are a counterparty-pays, pro-dissemination data source, in contrast to terminal data ($24-28k/seat/yr paid by consumers of data).

**Known conflicts / verify-before-quoting:** Cision revenue ($602M vs $826M estimates, both weak); Hudson Labs total funding ($1.22M vs $5.5M); Business Wire revenue ($257.7M, single aggregator); Bloomberg terminal count (325k dates to 2022 — no newer firm figure found); Quartr total funding ($23M vs $34.6M); FINRA 77% and hedge-fund 92% figures were relayed via a vendor (Hebbia) roundup, not fetched from primary.
