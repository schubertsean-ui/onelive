## Implications for a press-release/disclosure aggregation + longitudinal diff-analysis product

- **Ingestion risk is low, identity plumbing risk was on the filing side.** EDGAR Next changed authentication for *filers*, not consumers: data.sec.gov, submissions.json, full-text search, and the 10 req/s fair-access regime are intact. Build to the 10 req/s + declared User-Agent contract, but budget for change — Atkins' EDGAR legacy-system review means the read-side APIs could be modernized (or re-permissioned) within the 3–5 year window.
- **Earnings releases remain the biggest unstructured white space.** No US rule tags 8-K EX-99 earnings releases, and none is proposed — so NLP/LLM extraction over press releases retains durable value for at least 3–5 years in the US, while Japan's TDnet (Jul 2026) shows the direction of travel: once tagged, a diff product should switch to structured-first ingestion per jurisdiction.
- **FDTA's real payoff is entity resolution, not formats.** LEI as the cross-agency entity key (effective Oct 1, 2026, applied via agency rules over ~2 years) plus the FCA's NSM LEI metadata mandate make LEI the natural join key for longitudinal, cross-jurisdiction entity graphs; the format standard is principles-based, so don't expect a single US machine-readable format — FIGI/CUSIP instrument-level joins stay unresolved.
- **ESAP is a 2027–2028 aggregation opportunity and threat.** A free EU-wide portal + API with mandatory LEI-tagged, machine-readable submissions (financials 2027, CSRD sustainability from Jan 2028) commoditizes raw EU collection — differentiation shifts to longitudinal diffing, cross-filing linkage, and US/EU/UK/JP normalization that ESAP explicitly does not do.
- **Regulatory slippage is systematic — build timeline optionality.** Form SHO (→2028), N-PORT (→2027), broker-dealer structured data (→2029), Companies House iXBRL (2027→2028), FDTA final rule (~18 months late): assume announced structured-data start dates slip 12–24 months, and avoid roadmap bets that depend on a mandate landing on schedule.
- **Diff-analysis tailwind:** growing iXBRL coverage (SPAC items, proxy items, UKSEF narrative tags, TDnet quarters, Companies House 2028) means more disclosures become fact-level comparable over time — a longitudinal diff engine that operates at both text level (today's press releases) and tagged-fact level (expanding) rides the whole curve.
