## Implications for a press-release/disclosure aggregation + diff-analysis product

- **The incumbent distribution layer is financially fragile, not dead.** Cision (Caa-rated, distressed exchange, CEO churn, HARO divestiture) and the Notified/EQS take-unders show wires are PE-squeezed melting-ice-cube assets. Plan for consolidation, API/licensing instability, and possible PR Newswire ownership change within 3–5 years — do not build sourcing on a single wire's feed terms; ingest from EDGAR 8-Ks, IR pages, and multiple wires in parallel from day one.
- **Provenance/verification is a proven, monetizable feature with a real gap.** GlobeNewswire's CLEAR Verified and Business Wire's authentication marketing confirm demand post fake-Getty/Walmart-Litecoin incidents, but nobody is doing cryptographic signing or C2PA-style credentials for text disclosures. A diff-analysis product that verifies "this text matches the 8-K/IR-site canonical version, from a verified issuer, unaltered" is differentiated and rides a regulatory tailwind (SEC enforcement of fake releases).
- **AI slop makes filtering the value-add.** ~24% of wire releases are already LLM-assisted, and in crypto 62.5% of releases are tied to high-risk/scam projects; Google is actively demoting syndicated PR content. An aggregator's moat is trust-scoring and de-duplication (issuer verification, cross-source confirmation, AI-likelihood signals), not raw volume — mirrors your gate-before-publish architecture.
- **Diff-analysis fits the "wire bypass" trend.** JPMorgan/Goldman-style IR-site-first disclosure means material information increasingly appears as silent website/8-K changes rather than wire pushes. Monitoring IR pages + EDGAR for diffs (what changed vs. the prior release, wire copy vs. filed copy) captures exactly the content that leaves the wires — and becomes more valuable as wire coverage shrinks.
- **Pricing pain is a wedge.** $760+/release wire pricing rising ~$100/yr while ACCESS Newswire converts the low end to $1,000–2,500/mo subscriptions suggests issuers (esp. small/mid-cap) are price-sensitive; an aggregation product can position as the neutral reader-side layer that makes cheaper/direct disclosure channels credible (verification substituting for the wire's editorial imprimatur).
- **Risk to flag:** if wires respond by locking down content licensing (as their revenues fall), aggregation rights get costlier; prioritize public-record sources (SEC filings, regulatory news services with statutory dissemination duties, issuer-owned pages) as the legally durable substrate.
