# Place lookup — Google Places and Brave Place Search

Founder 2026-09-10: Google is trusted. Brave is the alternative. Both are look-ups for a Place. Neither creates a show.

## What they may fill

Photo, map point, street, phone, site — only when that field is still a hole.

Preferred order:

1. The venue’s own site (wins a disagreement).
2. Google listing page or Google Places look-up.
3. Brave Place Search look-up.
4. Yelp / Instagram / Facebook page, if that page states the field.
5. The act / group / person’s own site.

Act photo stays the event image a desk already printed (Ticketmaster `image_url`). Do not replace it with a venue shot.

## Rules

1. Look up when the card needs a missing photo or point. Do not copy the result into our catalog as if we own it.
2. Google ToS: keep their place ID only. Do not warehouse photos or addresses.
3. Brave ToS: do not store search results except a short display cache. Their place IDs expire in about 8 hours.
4. Neither API creates a happening. Title + when + a trusted activity door still required.
5. Do not invent a street or a pin. Do not print Brave or Google star ratings.
6. Map picture (city outline + river + mark) shows only when we already have a printed point. The picture clicks to Apple Maps or Google Maps.
7. Key + monthly spend cap are founder-crucial. No key in the repo as of 2026-09-10.

## Cost (Brave, public pricing 2026-07)

About $5 per 1,000 Place Search calls. $5 free credit per month.
