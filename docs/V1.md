## What v1 is

> **A stranger in Austin opens the site tonight and sees what is actually
> happening tonight — accurate, with honest uncertainty, refreshed without anyone
> touching it — and locks their phone within ten seconds holding a decision they
> feel good about.**

That is the whole product. Deliberately thin.

The second half of that sentence is not a flourish. It is `docs/BAR.md` row P1, it
is the brief's own payoff (§5), and it is the half that has never been measured.
A v1 that satisfies the first half and fails the second is a data pipeline with a
web page attached, not the product described in
`docs/design/ONE_LIVE_MASTER_DESIGN_BRIEF_v2.4.md`.

## What v1 is not

Not: matching, group plans, Tastemaker posts, the Emotion/Vibe layer, voice
personas, native mobile, the Meta carousel marketing engine, the promise-ledger
venture, the knowledge-graph brain. All of those are built or specified to some
degree and **none of them is v1**. They stay in the tree, frozen, and they do not
get another line of work until the seven criteria below are met.

## v1 is done when all seven are true

The founder directive of 2026-07-26 — *everything is built toward the vision and how
it is supposed to make people feel* — changed this list. It previously had five
criteria, **all mechanical**: data flows, tests green, deployed. Those are necessary
and they are not sufficient. A v1 that ingests perfectly and fails the fan on the
sidewalk is not a v1. Criteria 6 and 7 are the ones that were missing.

| # | Done-criterion | How it is proven | Bar row |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The feed refreshes itself.** Both deterministic importers complete on their schedules, watched by dead-man alarms. | two consecutive green scheduled runs of each, and a healthchecks check that alarms if they stop | C1, C2, H1, **P13** |
| 2 | **A passing long-tail candidate reaches users without a human click**, except the ratified exceptions (conflict / unreliable source / fabrication risk → human review). | a real candidate auto-published at its earned confidence, visible on `/tonight`, with the exception paths proven by test | C3, A1–A6, **P8** |
| 3 | **Master is green.** | `bash tools/validate` exits 0 with no SKIP unaccounted for | G1 |
| 4 | **The experience is measured**, not assumed: load time, INP, CLS and WCAG 2.2 AA on the deployed preview. | a CI run against the preview URL, numbers recorded | E1–E4, **P2** |
| 5 | **It is deployed and the URL is on disk.** | the URL in this file, `/api/health` returning `ok:true` with `eventCount>0` | H7 |
| 6 | **The ten-second answer works.** A person who has never seen the site, on a phone, with no account, reaches a decision they feel good about — timed. | ≥ 3 first-time users, timed on a real phone on the deployed URL, **median ≤ 10 s**, results recorded here. Founder may be one of them; at least two must not be. | **P1** |
| 7 | **The feeling is scored, not assumed.** The shipped feed is graded against the brief's own 8-criterion rubric — including emotional fidelity to §3 and the white-hat reflection test. | rubric scored against the deployed surface, **≥ 4/5 on every criterion**, deltas logged never silent | **P14, P4, P12** |

Note what is *not* on that list: no new feature, no new subsystem, no new
document. v1 is finished by connecting and verifying what exists — and then by
checking that what exists actually delivers the moment it was built for.

**Criterion 6 is deliberately cheap and deliberately human.** Three people, a
phone, a stopwatch. There is no instrumentation to build, no framework to adopt,
and no excuse for it having never been done.

## The order of work, and why this order

### Step 1 — the deterministic feed refreshes itself  *(in progress)*

**Why first:** it needs no AI, costs nothing per event, cannot hallucinate, and
it is the difference between a website and a demo. It also does not wait for the
Anthropic account to recover on 2026-08-01.

- **1a. DONE in this change.** `import_structured.yml` (venue / university /
  library / civic / museum calendars) failed on every scheduled run because its
  fetch bound came from `github.event.inputs.limit`, which is empty on a schedule
  event. Fixed, with `tests/test_scheduled_inputs_contract.py` now guarding the
  whole class across every scheduled workflow. *Verify by:* the next scheduled run
  at 08:17 or 20:17 UTC completing green.
- **1b. NEEDS THE FOUNDER (ask #1).** `import_licensed.yml` (Ticketmaster +
  SeatGeek — the bulk of the feed) has no schedule at all. Adding one requires a
  dead-man alarm per the charter, which needs one healthchecks.io check and one
  repository secret. Steps are in *Asks* below. Once the secret exists this is a
  15-line workflow change modelled exactly on `import_structured.yml`.

### Step 2 — a passing candidate publishes itself

**Why second:** it is the thing the founder explicitly asked for on 2026-07-25
and the only reason the long tail cannot reach users today. It is also the
largest remaining piece of real engineering.

The founder's ratification set three conditions. Honest status:

| Condition | Status | What it needs |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Source-reliability grading is real — scores updated from outcomes, so "graded unreliable" actually gates | **NOT MET** | nothing calls `worker/source_reliability.py` outside itself. An outcome-driven update on each run, persisted, with a test proving a degrading source falls below the threshold. |
| 2. Honest uncertainty display is live | **built, unproven end-to-end** | `web/lib/trust.ts` renders the quiet marker; confirm once auto-published rows flow through it |
| 3. No fabrication — sensor + schema guards stay in force | **MET** | unchanged |

Then: `worker/autopromote.py` — the only new promoter — reads
`worker/publish_policy.py`'s decision, is added to the promote-import allowlist in
the same change, and stays behind `AUTO_PUBLISH_RATIFIED` (fail-closed OFF). The
flag flips only after 1–3 are verified live on a preview.

**This is a trust-path change.** It goes through the mandatory independent review,
and the charter has been corrected in this change to stop contradicting the
ratified decision.

### Step 3 — master green

**Master is already green with full history — 1,665 tests, verified 2026-07-26.**
What is not fixed is that the agent's default environment manufactures four red
rows out of 19 that mean "your environment is incomplete," not "your code is
broken": three from missing interpreter packages, one from a shallow clone making
the arming binding unprovable (it fails closed by design — R-036). Presented in
the same red column as a real regression, they train everyone to scan past red.

So Step 3 is: a dev-dependency file plus a documented one-command bootstrap
(including full history), and `tools/validate` reporting `MISSING-TOOL` /
`UNPROVABLE-HERE` separately from `FAIL`. **The exit code does not change and no
check is loosened** — the gate still fails closed; it just stops lying about why.
(Audit D7 and D12, R-058.)

### Step 4 — deploy and record the URL

`docs/DEPLOY.md` documents a zero-config preview path: a Vercel preview needs no
environment variables at all, the gate auto-opens on preview (host-protected by
Vercel), `/ops` stays denied, and the Supabase read uses the committed public
publishable key. Deploy, hit `/api/health`, paste the URL into this file.

### Step 5 — measure the experience (machine half)

A Lighthouse + axe run in CI against the preview URL, recording load time, INP and
CLS at p75 and a WCAG 2.2 AA pass. Today **nothing** measures the part users
actually touch (bar rows E1–E4, P2). The product bar for load is **2.0 s** — the
brief's number — with Core Web Vitals' 2.5 s as the floor of external
acceptability. The stricter number wins; that is a tightening, not a relaxation.

### Step 6 — the ten-second answer, timed on a real phone

**This is the step that has never been attempted, and it is the cheapest one on the
list.** Bar row P1 is the most important number in the product — a stranger
answering "what should I do tonight?" in under ten seconds — and it has never been
measured once, in any form, by anyone.

What it takes: three people who have not seen the site, one phone, a stopwatch, the
deployed URL. Start the clock when the page opens; stop it when they say what
they're doing tonight. Median ≤ 10 s. Write the three numbers into this file.

At least two of the three must not be the founder — the person who designed the
information architecture cannot measure how legible it is to someone who didn't.

Expect it to fail the first time. That is the point: it is the only step on this
list that can tell us the product works as *a product* rather than as a pipeline.

### Step 7 — score the feeling against the brief's own rubric

The brief carries its own 8-criterion rubric (PART C) and it has only ever been
applied to design *comps*, never to a shipped surface. Score the deployed feed:
ten-second answer · night-sidewalk legibility · trust-by-craft with zero badges ·
distinctiveness against the named competitors · **emotional fidelity to §3
(anticipation + calm certainty)** · accessibility evident · survivability ·
click-pull passing the white-hat reflection test.

≥ 4/5 on every criterion. Deltas logged, never silent. A criterion scoring below 4
is a v1 blocker, not a note for later — because the feeling is the spec
(`CLAUDE.md` prime directive 5).
