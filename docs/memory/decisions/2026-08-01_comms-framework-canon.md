# Decision: the five-part communication framework enters canon (founder-ratified)

**Date:** 2026-08-01 · **Status:** RATIFIED · **Decider:** founder

Verbatim anchors (same conversation, in order):
1. "go easy on the marketing spiel - just stick to the what, how and why,
   why that why matters and expected outcomes."
2. "make this part of the canon and modify if instructed: stick to the
   what, how and why, why that why matters and expected outcomes."

**What is canon:** research papers, product explainers, and
partner/founder-facing materials are structured on the five-part
framework, in this order and with this naming:

1. **WHAT** — the thing itself, stated plainly.
2. **HOW** — the mechanism.
3. **WHY** — the grounded reason (research/evidence, not slogans).
4. **WHY THAT WHY MATTERS** — the consequence of the reason; what changes
   because it is true.
5. **EXPECTED OUTCOMES** — concrete, measurable, honestly labeled
   (estimates marked as estimates).

Marketing spiel — slogans, superlatives, applause lines — is excluded
from these materials. Owner-facing PRODUCT surfaces (e.g., the agent's
conversational threads) keep their warm plain voice; this framework
governs analysis and explanatory documents.

**Modification:** only on founder instruction ("modify if instructed") —
agents do not restyle away from this framework on their own judgment.
