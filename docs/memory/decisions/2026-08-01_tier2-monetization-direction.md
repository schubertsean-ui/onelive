# Decision: Tier-2 monetization direction (founder-directed)

**Date:** 2026-08-01 · **Status:** DIRECTION RATIFIED (model/rates/timing open) · **Decider:** founder

Verbatim anchor: "'no percentage taken' - this is true for the initial
set up but we may want to find a way to monetize the ongoing work after
some period of time, whether flat fee/month or %, because this is truly
'extra' value being delivered and at lower rates than they would
otherwise pay since this is work they either don't do (83%) and would
have to pay someone to do it, let alone do it well as as efficiently."

**What this changes:**
1. The free-forever commitment is SCOPED, as the Mantle already framed
   it: **Tier 1 (maintenance & standards — the basics/table stakes) is
   free permanently.** That public promise is unchanged.
2. **Tier 2 (ongoing demand generation — campaign production,
   distribution, offers, measurement) is free for an initial period,
   then MAY be priced** — flat monthly fee or percentage-based — at
   rates below the market alternatives documented in the research
   ($300–$5,000/mo), on the grounds the founder states: it is extra
   value most of the segment (83% invisible in AI answers) does not
   perform today and would otherwise buy at higher cost and lower
   quality.
3. Prior doc language reading "no percentage taken" as a blanket
   permanent rule is superseded to: no percentage of their AD SPEND is
   taken at setup or during the free period; ongoing Tier-2 pricing per
   this direction.

**What does NOT change (physics):** nothing paid or free ever affects
OneLive ranking, ordering, display, or trust states (the Studio
corollary, Q22); the agent remains a gated source, never a publisher;
every send stays owner-tapped; leaving preserves everything built.

**Open for the Phase-B pricing packet (founder decides with data):**
the model (flat vs %), what any % is computed ON (attributable tracked
revenue vs ad spend — noting the research flagged spend-percentage as
an incentive-misaligned agency pattern), the rate, the length of the
initial free period, grandfathering, and segment/tier variations.
Queued in TODOS with the existing Mirror decision packet.
