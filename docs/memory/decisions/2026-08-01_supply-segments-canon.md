# Decision: the 23 supply-side segments + 5 size tiers enter canon (founder-ratified)

**Date:** 2026-08-01 · **Status:** RATIFIED · **Decider:** founder

Verbatim anchors (same conversation, in order):
1. "separate bars from wineries, breweries and distilleries" (splitting the
   original 22-category proposal's category 2)
2. "they also sell tastings and sales or their products and education"
   (the producers category's three-sided revenue model: visit · product ·
   experience/education)
3. "let's add to the canon the 23 segments and add this content"

**What is canon:** the segmentation itself — the 23 supply-side categories
(11 Venues & Places · 6 Organizers & Groups · 6 Artists & People) and the
five size tiers (SOLO nonemployer · SMALL 1–9 · MEDIUM 10–49 · LARGE
50–249 · JUMBO 250+, per OECD bands + the Census nonemployer split).
Canonical doc: `docs/strategy/ONE_LIVE_SUPPLY_SEGMENTS_v1.md`.

**What is NOT canon (research-grade, validate-by-conversation):** the
per-category briefs' challenges/goals/recommendations
(`ONE_LIVE_CATEGORY_RESEARCH_23_v1.md`) are sourced-or-labeled hypotheses
feeding the outreach conversation program; they update with evidence and
never harden without it. The marketing-operating analysis
(`ONE_LIVE_MARKETING_OPERATING_REALITY_v1.md`) is sourced research with
labeled expert estimates.

**Scope note:** this is a research/vocabulary canon (how we segment and
speak about supply). It changes no trust physics, no pipeline behavior,
and no product surface by itself; any schema/product use of the taxonomy
goes through normal gates when built.
