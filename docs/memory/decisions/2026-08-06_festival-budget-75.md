# 2026-08-06 — Festival-window search budget raised to $75/month

## Founder directive (verbatim)

> Yes to $46 budget - go to $75 to ensure no issues.

## What was asked

October 2026 puts two festival windows live in the same month (ACL and F1),
and the phased festival-window sweeps (`tools/festival_phase.py`, PR #187)
were projected at roughly **$46** for that month against a standing **$40**
tripwire. The agent escalated it as a money decision rather than absorbing it.

## The ruling

**Approved, and the ceiling is raised to $75/month** — deliberately above the
projection rather than at it, so a heavier-than-modelled festival month does
not trip the alarm and stall sweeps mid-window. The founder's stated intent is
"to ensure no issues": headroom is the point, not a target to spend to.

## How this binds

- The tripwire moves $40 → **$75/month** for the search-lane festival sweeps.
- $75 is a CEILING, not a budget to consume. The $46 projection remains the
  expected spend; the extra $29 is headroom for an underestimate.
- Crossing $75 is a new escalation, not a second automatic raise.
- The console-level spend caps on the extraction key are untouched by this —
  they are a separate control ($500/mo, alert at $280, founder-set).

## Cost discipline still applies

The charter's rule that every stage uses the cheapest-capable tier is
unaffected. Headroom exists so a festival window does not stall; it is not a
licence to route a stage to a more expensive model because the ceiling allows
it. Cost-per-verified-event remains the measure.
