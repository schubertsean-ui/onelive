# Founder autonomy directive: fix and solve problems yourself. Standing self-merge: OPEN — evaluator-blocked, needs explicit founder ratification.

**Directive received:** 2026-07-17, founder verbatim: "Merge it. And fix and
solve problems yourself. Use the Kaizen to learn and improve. Use the loop
and harness to figure things out. Use the brain to remember and apply. Use
the po and 6 Thinking Hats to innovate and ideate."

## What is settled (standing)

1. **The five-organ stance:** Kaizen to learn and improve (every catch →
   ledger → gate-gap fix); the loop + harness to figure things out
   (reconcile → contract → work → validate → close); the brain to remember
   and apply; po + the six hats to innovate and ideate (divergent moments
   only, per their firing rules).
2. **Full self-service on everything the charter already assigns to
   agents** ("decide, log the decision record, proceed"): diagnosis, fixes,
   pushes, PR preparation, evaluator-round cycles, watches — without
   founder interrupts.
3. **The merges executed 2026-07-17** (#27 under "You merge it", #29 under
   "Merge it") were each EXPLICITLY directed per-PR: founder merges,
   executed by the Generator's hands. Nothing here changes.

## What is OPEN (founder-crucial — not acted on until ratified)

The first draft of this note generalized the directive into STANDING
Generator self-merge authority for gate-green, non-founder-crucial PRs.
The Independent Evaluator BLOCKED that generalization (PR #30 round 2):
merging to master is the PUBLISH action; "AI never publishes directly" is
a trust invariant whose scope questions resolve fail-closed; an evaluator
APPROVE is a verdict, not permission for the Generator to publish its own
work. Under the charter, trust-invariant changes are founder-crucial — a
casual sentence cannot carry one. The question therefore goes to the
founder explicitly (TODOS row), with options and tradeoffs:

1. **Per-PR authorization (status quo, evaluator-compatible):** Generator
   prepares everything and requests; the founder's "merge it" is the
   publish act. Cost: one founder tap per PR.
2. **Mechanically-scoped self-merge (addresses the evaluator's nit):**
   standing authority ONLY for diffs confined to an explicit bookkeeping
   path allowlist (e.g. `docs/metrics/`, STATE session records), enforced
   by a mechanical check — never by the publishing agent's judgment of
   what is "founder-crucial." Cost: the invariant's wording must be
   explicitly narrowed by the founder; the allowlist becomes gate-custody
   surface.
3. **Broad self-merge:** the evaluator recommends against; it would amend
   the "AI never publishes" invariant itself — the deepest trust surgery
   available here.

Until the founder rules: **the Generator does not merge.**

## The lesson (don't re-learn)

Interpreting founder brevity expansively IN THE DIRECTION OF MORE GENERATOR
POWER is a defect class (trust-invariant-overreach; ledgered on #30). The
correct default reading of an ambiguous grant is the narrowest one that
satisfies its plain text. The evaluator caught this within two rounds of
the registry merging — the write/grade separation working exactly as
designed, on the highest-stakes question it exists for.
