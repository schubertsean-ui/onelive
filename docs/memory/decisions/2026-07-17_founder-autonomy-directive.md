# Founder autonomy directive: fix and solve problems yourself; the harness organs are the how.

**Ratified:** 2026-07-17, founder verbatim: "Merge it. And fix and solve
problems yourself. Use the Kaizen to learn and improve. Use the loop and
harness to figure things out. Use the brain to remember and apply. Use the
po and 6 Thinking Hats to innovate and ideate."

## The decision

1. **Self-service on non-founder-crucial work, including merges.** A PR that
   is (a) gate-green (trust-gate + independent evaluator APPROVE), and
   (b) not founder-crucial in content (charter escalation list: money/new
   services, legal posture, trust-invariant changes, gate-threshold
   relaxations, go-live/allowlist, credential minting) may be merged by the
   Generator without a founder interrupt. Context: the founder had been
   personally clicking merge on evaluator-approved bookkeeping PRs (#27,
   #29) — this directive ended that.
2. **The founder-crucial escalation list is unchanged.** This directive
   widens agent authority over everything the charter already said agents
   decide ("decide, log the decision record, proceed") — it does not touch
   the interrupt list. When in doubt whether content is founder-crucial,
   it is.
3. **The five-organ operating stance, standing:** Kaizen to learn and
   improve (every catch → ledger → gate-gap fix); the loop + harness to
   figure things out (reconcile → contract → work → validate → close);
   the brain to remember and apply (this note is itself compliance);
   po + the six hats to innovate and ideate (divergent moments only,
   per their firing rules).

## Why this matters (don't re-learn)

Before this note, "founder merges" was an unwritten habit, not a rule — no
charter text required it, and it cost a founder interrupt per bookkeeping
PR. The directive aligns practice with the charter's actual interrupt
boundary.

## Boundary

Self-merge authority is conditional on the gates: a red or unreviewed PR is
never self-merged, and using this directive to merge anything on the
founder-crucial list would be a trust violation, not autonomy.
